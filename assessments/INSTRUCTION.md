# AI Engineer Technical Assessment

Welcome, aspiring AI Engineer!

This technical assessment is designed to evaluate your skills relevant to the AI Engineer role at our company. We're looking for individuals who are passionate about building intelligent systems and solving real-world challenges using AI.

## 🚀 Role Context

As an AI Engineer, you will be responsible for designing, implementing, training, and deploying artificial intelligence models. This role involves integrating AI solutions into existing systems, collaborating with data scientists and machine learning engineers, and ensuring the performance and scalability of AI-driven products.

## ⏱️ Assessment Duration & Time Limit

This assessment is designed to be completed within **1 week (maximum 72 hours of focused work)**. We understand you may have other commitments, so manage your time effectively.

## 📝 How to Participate

1.  **Fork this Repository:** Start by forking this GitHub repository to your personal account.
2.  **Clone Your Fork:** Clone your forked repository to your local machine.
3.  **Create a New Branch:** Before starting any work, create a new branch named `solution-your-name` (e.g., `solution-john-doe`). All your work should be committed to this branch.
4.  **Locate Your Tasks:** You will find your tasks within the `submissions/week-1/` directory. Each task has its own subdirectory with an `Instruction.md` file.
5.  **Develop Your Solution:** Implement your solutions within the respective task directories. Ensure all necessary files, code, documentation, and tests are included.
6.  **Commit Your Changes:** Make regular, descriptive commits as you progress.
7.  **Submit Your Work:** Once you have completed all tasks, push your `solution-your-name` branch to your forked repository and open a **single Pull Request** to the `main` branch of the original assessment repository. Please ensure your PR description includes any necessary instructions for running your code or reviewing your design.

## 🌟 Evaluation Criteria

Your submission will be evaluated based on the following:

*   **Code Quality (25%):** Readability, maintainability, adherence to best practices, modularity, and proper commenting.
*   **Testing (20%):** Adequacy of unit/integration tests, test coverage, and clarity of test cases.
*   **Performance (20%):** Efficiency of your algorithms and solutions, considering computational resources and time complexity (where applicable).
*   **Documentation (20%):** Clarity and completeness of explanations, design choices, setup instructions, and API documentation (if any).
*   **Accessibility (15%):** User-friendliness and ease of understanding for APIs, interfaces, or generated outputs (where applicable).

A passing score of **75%** is required.

## 💡 Important Notes

*   **Do not submit solutions via email or any other channel.** Only Pull Requests will be considered.
*   **Use Python** as the primary language for coding tasks, leveraging libraries like TensorFlow or PyTorch as appropriate.
*   Feel free to use any standard libraries or frameworks you deem necessary, but clearly document their usage.
*   We encourage you to write clean, well-structured, and well-tested code.
*   If you have any clarifying questions, please reach out to the hiring team.

Good luck, and we look forward to seeing your innovative solutions!
